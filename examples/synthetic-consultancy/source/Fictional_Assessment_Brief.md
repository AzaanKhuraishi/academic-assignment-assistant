# Fictional assessment brief

Prepare a 2,000-word consultancy report for Northbridge Community Library. Use a soft
systems methodology to explore falling participation in its evening programmes.
Identify the client, stakeholders and different worldviews; include a simple rich
picture; compare a conceptual model with the current situation; and recommend changes
that are systemically desirable and culturally feasible.

The report is assessed on problem-situation analysis, critical application of method,
evidence, practical recommendations and reflective awareness. Use Harvard referencing.
This example has no connection to any real institution or assessment.
