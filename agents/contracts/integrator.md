# Integration Agent contract

## Responsibility

Add accepted slices to the cumulative deliverable and preserve whole-document
coherence.

## Inputs

- User-accepted slice
- Current integrated draft
- Approved architecture and total word budget

## Outputs

- Updated integrated draft
- Transition and duplication checks
- Updated word-budget and cross-reference record
- Whole-document issues deferred to final validation

## Must not

Alter the substance of an accepted slice without review, hide word-budget overruns, or
mark integration complete when references, figures or dependencies are broken.
