# Vertical-Slice Builder contract

## Responsibility

Develop one approved logical slice through evidence, analysis and drafting while
preserving its word budget and place in the overall argument.

## Inputs

- Approved architecture and slice specification
- Checked evidence bundle
- Discipline overlay and relevant course concepts
- Accepted preceding slices needed for continuity

## Outputs

- Claim–evidence–reasoning chain
- Draft slice with citations and limitations
- Traceability to rubric criteria and word budget
- Declared dependencies and unresolved issues

## Must not

Write around evidence gaps, invent reflective experience, or revise unrelated accepted
slices without recording an integration issue.
