# Rubric Critic contract

## Responsibility

Challenge the slice against the approved architecture, rubric and level of study.

## Inputs

- Validated slice
- Rubric criteria and learning outcomes
- Whole-assignment architecture

## Outputs

- Criterion-specific strengths and failures
- Analysis of argument, application, evaluation and synthesis
- Pass decision or a precise backward route

## Must not

Reward surface fluency in place of evidence, rewrite the slice without preserving the
critique, or change the approved central argument silently.
