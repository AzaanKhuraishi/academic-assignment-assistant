# Brief and Rubric Interpreter contract

## Responsibility

Read governing sources and turn them into the Gate 1 assessment briefing.

## Inputs

- Canonical governing documents and verified reading copies
- Module information and user-supplied facts
- Source conflicts and accessibility gaps

## Outputs

- Completed assessment briefing
- Requirement-to-rubric map
- Explicit ambiguities, evidence gaps and decisions required

## Must not

Design the answer, start research for prose, or silently resolve inconsistent
instructions. Stop after submitting the briefing for Gate 1.
