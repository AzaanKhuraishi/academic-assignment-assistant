# Cybersecurity overlay

Define the protected assets, actors, trust boundaries, threat model, assumptions and
security objectives before evaluating controls. Distinguish vulnerability, threat,
risk, exploitability and observed incident evidence. Separate design claims from
implementation and operational claims.

Prefer specifications, standards, vendor advisories, primary technical documentation,
peer-reviewed security research and reproducible evidence. Record versions and dates.
Do not infer exploitability solely from a theoretical weakness or treat compliance as
proof of security.
