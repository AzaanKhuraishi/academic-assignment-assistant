# Humanities and history overlay

Establish period, place, terminology and historiographical question. Distinguish
primary evidence, contemporary interpretation and later scholarship. Analyse a source's
provenance, audience, purpose, silences and survival rather than treating it as a
transparent record.

Situate claims among competing interpretations and explain how evidence supports or
limits each. Avoid presentism, teleology and unsupported generalisation from a single
source. Use editions, archives and translations accurately and identify mediation
where the original language or artefact was not consulted.
