# Consultancy and management overlay

## Required distinctions

- Client, beneficiary, problem owner and other stakeholders
- Presenting issue versus underlying problem situation
- Evidence, stakeholder perception and consultant inference
- Systemically desirable versus culturally feasible change
- Recommendation, implementation responsibility and success measure

Where Soft Systems Methodology is required, preserve the traceability chain:

`problem situation → worldview → purposeful activity → conceptual model → comparison with reality → feasible/desirable change → recommendation → implementation/evaluation`

Rich pictures should show structures, processes, relationships, conflicts, concerns
and environmental constraints. Conceptual models describe logically connected
purposeful activities, not the organisation as it currently operates.

Preferred evidence includes client material, stakeholder evidence, course-prescribed
methods, peer-reviewed scholarship, authoritative statistics and relevant professional
standards. Flag recommendations that lack ownership, sequencing, feasibility, risk or
measurement.
