# Vertical-slice method

A slice is a coherent contribution to the assignment's argument, not necessarily a
template heading. It may produce a section, analytical finding, recommendation chain,
figure or integrated combination of these.

Every slice records:

- Its purpose and rubric criteria
- Its word budget
- Research questions and evidence bundle
- Claims, limitations and unresolved gaps
- Draft and validation artefacts
- User feedback, retry routes and acceptance
- Integration location

The architect defines all slices together so the assignment remains coherent. The
builder completes one slice end-to-end so evidence and reasoning are validated before
large amounts of prose accumulate.
