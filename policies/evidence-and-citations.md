# Evidence and citation policy

Every material factual claim must be traceable to a checked source. Record the claim,
source, relevant finding, strength, limitation and citation status. Prefer primary,
peer-reviewed, official or otherwise authoritative evidence appropriate to the field.

Do not add a reference merely because it appears relevant. A source belongs in the
reference list only when it is cited, and every in-text citation must have a matching
entry. Verify quotations, page references, data and links against the authoritative
source. Label inference and disagreement between sources.
