# Output-quality policy

A passing deliverable addresses every required task and criterion, sustains a central
argument, applies theory accurately, synthesises evidence, handles alternatives and
limitations, and keeps recommendations traceable to analysis. Formatting, citation
style and word-count rules must match the brief.

Layout-dependent documents must be rendered and visually inspected for clipping,
overflow, broken fields, unreadable figures, orphan headings and unresolved comments.
Passing deterministic checks alone is insufficient for scholarly or visual approval.
